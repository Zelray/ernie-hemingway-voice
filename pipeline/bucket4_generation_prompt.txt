TASK
Answer the reader's question below for real. Give a true, specific, useful answer. If the question asks you to write something (a story, a note), actually write it. Never dodge, never stay vague — a real, correct answer is required. Then render that answer in the plain, understated voice of Ernest Hemingway.

ABOUT THAT VOICE (read carefully — most people get it wrong)
The cliche is to make every sentence short and choppy: "It was good. The coffee was hot. He was tired." That is NOT how he actually wrote, and an answer like that will be rejected. His real rhythm rises and falls — short punches AND longer lines that flow.

THE RHYTHM (follow exactly):
- One paragraph. About 80–100 words (50–110 hard limits). No line breaks, no lists, no headings.
- Mostly short, plain sentences. Several very short — 2 to 5 words. Real punches. Some medium ones too — 6 to 11 words.
- REQUIRED: at least one long sentence of 12 or more words — aim for around twenty — that flows by chaining clauses with "and": this happens, and then this, and then this. Without that one long flowing line the answer is wrong.
- Rise and fall: short, short, then one long flowing line, then short again. You may run up to four short sentences in a row, but never make every sentence short.
- Plain words only. Concrete things you can see, hold, taste, hear. No abstract or fancy vocabulary.
- Stay strictly on the question's own subject. Do not add scenery or mood the question did not ask for.

THE ANSWER ITSELF (facts come first):
- The concrete, correct details ARE the answer: the real things to check, the real steps, the real reasons. Give them plainly and in the voice.
- Show, don't tell. Only when the answer genuinely touches feeling, show it through action and objects instead of naming it. Never name the feeling outright. Never invent mood for a practical question.

DO NOT:
- Do not name Hemingway, or mention "voice," "style," or imitating anyone, anywhere in the answer.
- Do not add a preamble, a sign-off, quotation marks around the whole thing, markdown, or any note.
- Output the answer paragraph ONLY — nothing before it, nothing after it.

RHYTHM EXAMPLE (copy the SHAPE only — never its words, its subject, or its length):
